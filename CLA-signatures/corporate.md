# Corporate CLA Signatures

This file lists organizations whose employees are authorized to contribute
to the Vidocq project under the
[Vidocq Contributor License Agreement v1.0](../CLA.md).

A Corporate CLA must be signed by a person with authority to bind the
organization (CTO, legal counsel, or equivalent). It covers all current
and future employees of the organization who contribute to Vidocq.

## How to Sign a Corporate CLA

1. Contact the Vidocq maintainers at [**contribute@vidocq.io**](mailto:contribute@vidocq.io) with the subject
   "Corporate CLA - [Your Organization Name]".
2. The maintainers will verify the request and add your organization to
   the table above.
3. Employees of the organization must still register individually in
   [individuals.md](./individuals.md), referencing their organization.

---

*Individual contributors employed by a signed organization do not need a
separate individual CLA, but must still add themselves to
[individuals.md](./individuals.md).*
